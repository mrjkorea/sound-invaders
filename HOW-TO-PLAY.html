MRJ · listen & shoot

HOW TO PLAY

Same game on phone, tablet, classroom PC, and laptop. Listen to the word, then shoot the matching rock.

Listen

  • Word audio is a baked MP3 from the JSON pack. It repeats until you shoot.
  • No internet voice, no browser speak — offline files only.

Shoot

  • 2 shots per word set. Make them count.
  • Correct rock = points and a new wave. Wrong rock = oops (streak resets).
  • If the right rock slips past, you hear it again.

Pickups

  • Bump a space station = extra life (up to 5).
  • Grab ice, then tap ICE (or press I / C) to freeze rocks.

Lives & stages

  • 3 lives. Touching a word rock costs a life.
  • Stages never end — they get faster. Highest score wins. There is no fake YOU WIN.

Phone / tablet

  • Left half: SLIDE to steer.
  • Right half or the SHOOT button: fire.
  • ICE button lights up when you have a freeze.

Computer / classroom PC

  • Move: arrow keys or A D
  • Shoot: space / X / L (space also starts the game)
  • Ice: I or C
  • You can also click SHOOT / ICE with a mouse.

Word packs

Change words on the title screen (WORD PACK), or add ?pack=packs/short-vowels-v1.json to the URL. The engine loads JSON — the word list is not hardcoded.
